This is a quick variant of the traditional Japanese red bean paste _anko_ (餡子). One way of serving it is on toast with whipped cream.

 The proper way to make _anko_ is beautifully shown in the movie [_An_ (Sweet Bean)](https://cinemaforall.org.uk/films_type/sweet-bean/); there is a good recipe [on JustOneCookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com/how-to-make-anko-red-bean-paste/). It is similar to making jam. My recipe is much quicker and still delicious. I used blueberry syrup because that's what I had going, but any sugar syrup will work.

#### Ingredients 

For two slices of toast:

- 6 tablespoons of tinned of adzuki beans (about 200 g)
- 2 tablespoons of sugar syrup (mine is 90 g sugar per 100 ml)
- 2 slices of bread for toasting (in Japan, this read bean paste is served on really thick slices)
- 75 ml double cream  (cream with 50% fat)

#### Preparation

- Drain the liquid, wash the beans in a sieve  and let them leak out for a few minutes.
- Combine the beans and syrup in a bowl. Crush with a fork, mix well.
- Microwave for 2 minutes at 360 W.
- Mix again, let it cool (I used _au bain marie_ to speed it up).
- When the _anko_ is cool enough, whip the cream with an electric mixer.
- Toast the bread.
- Spread the bean paste on the toast, add a dollop of whipped cream and enjoy.
